# Day-rate calculator

This context turns a desired annual salary and work assumptions into a suggested freelance day rate and its supporting breakdown.

## Language

**Calculator Input**:
A desired annual salary together with bonus, benefits, holiday, sick or contingency, and non-billable assumptions.

**Calculator Values**:
A complete, valid set of calculator input values after missing or malformed assumptions have been replaced with defaults.

**Calculator Outcome**:
The result of evaluating calculator input: a calculated rate breakdown, correctable field problems, or values outside the reliable calculation range.

**Annual Compensation Target**:
The salary target adjusted for bonus and benefits.

**Estimated Billable Days**:
The working days available after holiday, sick or contingency, and non-billable time.

**Suggested Day Rate**:
The annual compensation target divided by estimated billable days, rounded up to the quoting increment.
